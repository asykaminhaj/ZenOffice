# ZenOffice Privacy

ZenOffice opens, edits and saves documents locally on your device. The app
does not upload your documents anywhere.

- **No analytics.** The usage-statistics code of the upstream desktop app is
  not part of the Android build.
- **No AI features.** The AI panel is hidden and disabled in this build.
- **All files access.** Used only to list documents in Documents/ZenOffice
  and to open files you choose. You can revoke it in Android settings; you can
  still open files through the system file picker.
- **Links.** Tapping a link inside a document opens it in your browser.

This document describes what the app is built to do. If you find behaviour
that differs, please open an issue.
